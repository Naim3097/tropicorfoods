What happened to Marmite?

Officially delisted

Unilever confirmed Marmite was withdrawn from Malaysia and Singapore in April 2026, citing long-running production problems.

Old stock, rising prices

Jars still floating around online are leftover stock, often listed at two to four times the old shelf price.

No return date

No comeback has been announced. Kitchens that rely on yeast extract need an answer that does not depend on one.

The same jar-on-the-shelf role, filled locally.

UMAMITE is a true yeast-extract spread, the same family of product as Marmite. Rich, dark and deeply savoury, it does the same work on hot buttered toast and in the kitchen, from Marmite chicken to stocks, gravies and marinades.

It is made by FEEGOH, the seasoning brand of Tropicor Foods, at our halal-certified facilities in Malaysia. Local production is the point: the supply problem that took Marmite off the shelf is the one thing UMAMITE does not have.

Why it holds up as the alternative

Made by a manufacturer, not a trader

Tropicor Foods has made seasonings in Malaysia for over 30 years. The same jar tastes the same next month and next year, which matters when it carries a signature dish.
