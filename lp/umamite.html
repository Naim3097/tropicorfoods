Before you ask

Is UMAMITE halal?

Yes. It is produced at our JAKIM halal-certified facilities in Malaysia under GMP and MESTI. Worth checking before you buy imported substitutes; some are beef based or carry no certification at all.

It is the same category of product, a concentrated yeast-extract spread, made to our own recipe. It plays the same role on toast and in cooking.
