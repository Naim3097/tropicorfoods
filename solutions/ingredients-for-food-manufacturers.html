Common questions

Can you blend to our existing specification?

Yes. Send your specification and target volumes. We confirm feasibility, produce trial batches and lock the spec before production.

What documentation do you provide?

Certificates of analysis, ingredient declarations, and nutritional data as agreed in the specification, plus halal certification documentation.

Do you handle both small and large production volumes?

Yes, from trial and pilot batches through to recurring industrial volumes across our two Malaysian facilities.

Can you help reformulate for clean label?

Yes. No-added-MSG and cleaner-label reformulation is one of our most requested projects. Our FEEGOH No Added MSG stock is an example of that work.

Tell us what you need to make.

Share your application, volumes and timeline. Our team responds within one working day with next steps, pricing direction and samples where available.
