About this product

Tropicor Strawberry Puree is processed from ripe strawberries into a smooth, full-fruit puree with a clean ingredient deck and naturally vivid colour. It is produced under our halal-certified quality system in Malaysia.

From smoothies to bakery fillings, it performs where fresh fruit can’t: stable in heat, consistent in sweetness and colour, and available all year round.
