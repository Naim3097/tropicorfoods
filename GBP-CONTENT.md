# Google Business Profile — ready-to-paste content
### Written for search visibility. Copy directly into the profile.

Keyword targets woven in naturally throughout: *halal food manufacturer Malaysia*,
*seasoning manufacturer Klang / Selangor*, *food ingredient supplier Malaysia*,
*OEM food manufacturer*, *private label*, *premix*, *fruit puree supplier*,
*halal yeast extract*, *foodservice supplier*.

Two rules while editing anything below:
1. Never claim UMAMITE is *manufactured* or *made in* Malaysia. Approved wording
   only: developed in Malaysia, finished and packed in Malaysia.
2. Never name the competitor spread brand.

## 1. Business description

Paste as-is. 721 characters, within the 750 limit.

```
Tropicor Foods is a halal-certified food ingredient and seasoning manufacturer based in Klang, Selangor, supplying businesses across Malaysia and 25 export markets for over 30 years.

We produce seasonings, premixes, sauces, fruit purees and functional food ingredients for restaurants, hotels, caterers, food manufacturers, distributors and retail brands. Our FEEGOH range includes chicken stock, ikan bilis, Cukup Enak multipurpose seasoning, brown sauce and UMAMITE yeast extract spread.

We also offer OEM and private label manufacturing, custom formulation, halal certification support and contract packing.

Facilities are JAKIM halal, GMP, MESTI and HACCP compliant. Contact us for samples, specifications and pricing.
```

Note the deliberate split: "we produce seasonings, premixes, sauces, fruit purees
and ingredients" is a manufacturing claim and is true. "Our FEEGOH range
includes… UMAMITE" is a range statement, not a manufacturing claim. Keep that
separation if you reword anything.

## 6. Review requests

Reviews are the hardest and highest-value item. Send after a smooth delivery,
a successful audit, or a completed OEM project.

**WhatsApp**
```
Hi [Name], thank you for working with us on [product/project]. If you have a moment, a short Google review would really help other food businesses find us. It takes about a minute: [review link]

Thank you from the team at Tropicor Foods.
```

**Email**
```
Subject: A quick favour, if you have a minute

Hi [Name],

Thank you for your continued business. We're building our presence on Google so more food businesses in Malaysia can find us.

If you've been happy with our products or service, a short review would mean a lot: [review link]

Anything you can mention about product consistency, documentation or responsiveness is especially helpful to other buyers deciding on a supplier.

Thanks again,
[Name]
Tropicor Foods
```

Get the review link from the Business Profile dashboard ("Ask for reviews").

**Reply to every review.** A short, specific thank-you on positive ones. On
negative ones, a calm reply naming what you'll do — the reply is read by future
buyers far more than by the reviewer.
